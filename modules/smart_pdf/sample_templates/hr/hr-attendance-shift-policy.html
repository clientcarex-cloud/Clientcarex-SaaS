Human Resources Policy

Attendance & Shift Policy

Policy No {{document_no}} Effective {{effective_date}} Version {{policy_version}} Review {{review_date}}

1. Purpose

This policy establishes clear and consistent expectations for attendance, punctuality and shift working at {{clinic_name}}. Because our services are delivered round the clock, disciplined attendance is essential to safe staffing, orderly handovers and uninterrupted patient care.

  • Applies to all employees whose roles are governed by fixed or rotating duty rosters.
  • Read together with the Leave Policy and the Employee Handbook.

2. Working Hours & Shift Rosters

  • Departments operate on defined shifts, typically morning, evening and night, published in advance through the duty roster.
  • Rosters are prepared by the department head and released before the start of each roster cycle so that staff can plan accordingly.
  • Employees must report for the shift assigned to them and may not swap shifts without prior written approval of the supervisor.
  • A reasonable overlap is expected at shift change to allow a complete clinical handover.

3. Grace Period & Late Marking

  • A grace period of up to ten minutes from the scheduled shift start is permitted for genuine, occasional delays.
  • Arrival beyond the grace period will be recorded as a late mark; three late marks in a calendar month will be treated as half a day's loss of pay.
  • Habitual late arrival, even within tolerance, may be reviewed as an attendance concern.

4. Weekly Offs & Holidays

  • Each employee is entitled to weekly offs as scheduled in the roster; offs may fall on any day of the week depending on service needs.
  • The organisation publishes an annual list of gazetted and restricted holidays applicable to the establishment.
  • Staff required to work on a declared holiday will be granted compensatory off or holiday pay as per the rules in force.

5. Overtime & Compensatory Off

  • Overtime must be authorised in advance by the reporting manager and recorded in the attendance system.
  • Approved overtime is compensated either through payment or compensatory off, in line with the applicable statutory provisions.
  • Compensatory off must ordinarily be availed within thirty days of accrual, subject to departmental staffing.

6. Biometric / Register Compliance

  • Every employee must personally record attendance through the approved biometric device or attendance register at the start and end of each shift.
  • Marking attendance on behalf of another employee (proxy attendance) is a serious act of misconduct.
  • Failure of a biometric punch must be reported immediately to the supervisor for manual regularisation with justification.

7. Consequences of Non-compliance

  • Unauthorised absence, proxy attendance and repeated late marking will attract action under the disciplinary procedure.
  • Attendance records directly influence salary processing, confirmation, increments and appraisal outcomes.
  • Persistent non-compliance may lead to warning, loss of pay or, in serious cases, termination of service.

Management reserves the right to amend, interpret or withdraw any provision of this policy, with changes communicated through official channels. This policy shall be read together with the other HR policies of {{clinic_name}}.
