The Parties wish to protect certain confidential and proprietary information disclosed to, or accessed by, the Employee in the course of employment. In consideration of the Employee's engagement and access to such information, the Parties agree as follows:

  1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, disclosed to or accessed by the Employee, including but not limited to:
    • patient records and protected health information, including medical histories, diagnoses, treatment plans and test results;
    • clinical protocols, research data, case studies and quality-assurance materials;
    • financial information, pricing, billing data, contracts and commercial terms;
    • operational information, including systems, processes, staff data, vendor arrangements and business strategies.
  2. Obligations of Confidentiality. The Employee shall: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it solely for the legitimate performance of assigned duties; (c) not disclose, publish, copy, transmit or reproduce it to or for any third party without prior written authorisation; and (d) apply at least the same degree of care to protect it as the Employer applies, and in all events not less than a reasonable degree of care, in accordance with applicable data-protection and medical-privacy laws.
  3. Exclusions. The obligations herein shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach by the Employee; (b) was lawfully known to the Employee prior to disclosure without an obligation of confidence; (c) is lawfully received from a third party entitled to disclose it; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation or a competent authority, provided the Employee gives the Employer prompt written notice and cooperates in seeking protective measures.
  4. Return of Materials. Upon cessation of employment, or upon the Employer's request at any time, the Employee shall promptly return or, at the Employer's option, securely destroy all documents, devices, records, copies and materials containing Confidential Information, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing.

Accordingly, the Employer shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law, including disciplinary action up to and including termination, and recovery of damages, costs and legal expenses.
  7. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the applicable laws in force, and the competent courts of the jurisdiction in which the Employer's registered office is situated shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising hereunder.
