Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

Policy No {{document_no}} Effective {{effective_date}} Version {{policy_version}} Review {{review_date}}

1. Policy Statement & Scope

{{clinic_name}} is committed to providing a safe, respectful and dignified working environment for every individual, free from sexual harassment. This policy is framed in keeping with the principles of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013.

  • Applies to all employees, trainees, interns, contract staff, consultants and visitors at every premises of the organisation.
  • Covers conduct at the workplace and at any location visited during the course of employment, including offsite events and official travel.
  • The organisation adopts a zero-tolerance approach to sexual harassment in any form.

2. Definition of Sexual Harassment

  • Unwelcome physical contact and advances, or a demand or request for sexual favours.
  • Sexually coloured remarks, jokes or gestures, whether spoken, written or electronic.
  • Showing or circulating pornographic or sexually offensive material.
  • Any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature that creates a hostile or intimidating environment.

3. Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

  • The organisation has constituted an Internal Complaints Committee to receive and enquire into complaints of sexual harassment.
  • The Committee is headed by a senior woman employee as Presiding Officer, with internal members and an external member from a body familiar with issues relating to sexual harassment.
  • At least one half of the members of the Committee are women.
  • Contact details of the Committee members are displayed at prominent locations and are available with the Human Resources department.

4. Complaint & Redressal Procedure

  • An aggrieved person may submit a written complaint to the ICC, ordinarily within three months of the incident, with an extension permitted for valid reasons.
  • The Committee may attempt conciliation at the request of the complainant before initiating a formal enquiry.
  • The enquiry is conducted following the principles of natural justice, giving both parties a fair opportunity to be heard.
  • The Committee endeavours to complete the enquiry and submit its report ordinarily within ninety days.

5. Confidentiality & Protection against Retaliation

  • The identity of the complainant, respondent, witnesses and the details of proceedings are kept strictly confidential.
  • No person shall victimise or retaliate against a complainant or witness for participating in the process in good faith.
  • Interim measures, such as transfer or leave, may be recommended to protect the complainant during the enquiry.

6. Disciplinary Action

  • If the allegation is proved, appropriate action will be taken in accordance with the service rules, which may include warning, transfer, withholding of increments or termination of service.
  • Where a complaint is found to be malicious or made with knowingly false information, action may be taken against the complainant.
  • Nothing in this policy prevents the aggrieved person from pursuing remedies available under the law.

7. Awareness & Training

  • The organisation conducts periodic orientation and sensitisation programmes for all employees and Committee members.
  • This policy is communicated to every new joiner as part of induction.
  • Awareness material is displayed at conspicuous places to keep the reporting mechanism visible and accessible.

Management is committed to upholding this policy in letter and spirit. It reserves the right to amend or interpret its provisions consistent with the governing law, with changes communicated through official channels.
